The Delhi Metro

The Delhi Metro is one of the fastest-growing transit networks in the world, expanding from zero to nearly 300 stations in just two decades. Let's travel through its history together!

Phase I: The Foundation

This phase was about proving the concept and connecting the absolute core of the city. The opening of the first section of the Red Line in 2002 was quickly followed by the Yellow and Blue lines.

Phase II: Commonwealth Games

Driven by the looming deadline of the 2010 Commonwealth Games, the metro underwent a massive, rapid expansion to connect the NCR suburbs like Noida and Gurgaon to the city center.

Phase III: The Great Rings

With the spokes built, the network desperately needed to connect them. The massive circular Pink and Magenta lines were constructed, intersecting older lines and drastically increasing the number of interchange stations.

Phase IV & Modern Era

Navigating global challenges and optimizing the vast network. After a massive drop during COVID-19, the network is completing its newer, smaller line extensions.
